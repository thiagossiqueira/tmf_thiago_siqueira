TAKEAWAY
The final results are not fragile to using DK lag(2) instead of lag(4).
Point estimates, signs and estimation samples are identical.
Lag(2) generally produces smaller standard errors and stronger statistical
significance. No statistically significant result is weakened or loses
significance under lag(2).

The frozen lag(4) specification is therefore the more conservative inference
choice in this comparison.
