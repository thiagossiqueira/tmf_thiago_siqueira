TFM WP-06C - Multi-Source Bond Volume Coverage Audit

OBJECTIVE
Assess whether alternative Bloomberg pricing sources can extend the
historical coverage of the WP-06B bond-liquidity measure beyond the
B3CT usable window.

BASELINE PRESERVED
WP-06B Candidate v1 remains unchanged.
Source: Bloomberg PX_VOLUME
pricing_source = B3CT
fill = NA
verified_trade_day = 1 iff PX_VOLUME > 0
eligible day = cdr_anbima business day within [ISSUE_DT, MATURITY)
bond_trading_day_share =
    verified_trading_days / eligible_trading_days
Frozen usable window = Sep-2023 to Jun-2025.

SOURCE UNIVERSE
26 pricing sources from pcs_debentures.xlsx:
ANBE, ANDE, ATIV, B3CT, B3CY, BDBR, BGN, BINT, BRAD, BSFE,
BVAL, CMDB, FX1L, GDEO, ICOR, JGPA, LCPR, MIAE, NECT, NUIN,
OPEA, PSAN, TPBZ, TRUS, VRTX, XPCP.

SCREEN RESULT
Only three sources returned positive PX_VOLUME:
B3CT : 25,347 positive bond-days; 91 bonds; first 2019-09-13.
B3CY : 19,969 positive bond-days; 87 bonds; first 2023-08-10.
FX1L :    333 positive bond-days;  9 bonds; first 2014-07-21.

MATCHED PRICE-SOURCE TEST
ANDE, ATIV, BRAD, CMDB and PSAN returned no positive PX_VOLUME
for the thesis bonds.

B3CY VS B3CT
For the B3CY-covered bonds, every B3CY positive trade-day was also
positive in B3CT.
Incremental B3CY binary trade-days = 0.
Therefore B3CY does not extend liquidity coverage.

FX1L VS B3CT
FX1L positive trade-days = 333.
Also positive in B3CT = 7.
FX1L-only incremental trade-days = 326.
Bonds with incremental FX1L observations = 9.
Incremental date range = 2014-07-21 to 2022-09-15.

All 326 incremental FX1L observations fall within
[ISSUE_DT, MATURITY):
before ISSUE_DT = 0
on/after MATURITY = 0.

CROSS-SECTIONAL COVERAGE GATE
Using the number of bonds present in the thesis panel each month:

Pre-Sep-2023 combined B3CT+FX1L coverage:
median = 10.0%
mean   = 13.2%

Months with coverage >= 10%: 77
Months with coverage >= 20%: 50
Months with coverage >= 25%: 42
Months with coverage >= 30%: 14
Months with coverage >= 40%: 1
Months with coverage >= 50%: 1
Months with coverage >= 75%: 1
Months with coverage >= 90%: 1

The sole month above 40% is Aug-2023:
38 / 42 panel bonds = 90.5%.

Aug-2023 remains rejected because the B3CT structural feed expansion
occurred during the month (10-11 Aug 2023). The first complete usable
month remains Sep-2023.

Pre-expansion examples:
Sep-2019: 9 / 25 bonds = 36.0%.
Oct-2019: 9 / 24 bonds = 37.5%.

CONCLUSION
FX1L provides genuine additional historical evidence of trading activity
for 9 thesis bonds and adds 326 binary trade-days not observed in B3CT.

However, this historical coverage is too sparse and cross-sectionally
unstable to support extending the econometrically usable liquidity window.

No multi-source Candidate v2 is adopted.

FINAL LIQUIDITY DECISION
Retain WP-06B Candidate v1 unchanged:
pricing source = B3CT
usable window = Sep-2023 to Jun-2025.

FX1L is retained only as documented supplementary historical evidence,
not as part of the final bond_trading_day_share variable.
