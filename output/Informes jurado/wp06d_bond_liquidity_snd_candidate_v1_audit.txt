LIQUIDITY DEFINITION
verified_trade_day = 1 iff SND reports a secondary-market transaction
for the bond on that date.

eligible day = cdr_anbima business day within [ISSUE_DT, MATURITY).

bond_trading_day_share_snd =
    verified_trading_days_snd / eligible_trading_days_snd

This reproduces the denominator definition used in frozen WP-06B.

NO-TRADE CONVENTION
For an eligible bond-month with no SND transaction:
    verified_trading_days_snd = 0
    bond_trading_day_share_snd = 0
    total_reported_quantity_snd = missing
    total_reported_trades_snd = missing

Thus, an observed absence of trading is treated as zero liquidity rather
than missing data.

SOURCE-SCOPE CAVEAT
The legacy SND/CETIP transaction records represent registered
secondary-market activity and are not restricted to REUNE extragroup
transactions.

Published Brazilian market research notes that CETIP transaction data
may contain both intragroup and extragroup operations, whereas REUNE
can be filtered to extragroup activity. Accordingly, the WP-06D
variable should be interpreted as the share of eligible business days
on which registered secondary-market trading activity was observed,
not specifically as the share of days with arm's-length extragroup
trading.

This distinction is particularly important for volume- or turnover-based
liquidity measures. WP-06D deliberately uses only a binary trading-day
indicator and does not interpret reported quantity or number of trades
as an econometric liquidity variable.

Scope references:
- BNDES, Revista do BNDES 44, discussion of secondary-market debenture
  liquidity using ANBIMA/CETIP/CVM data and the distinction between
  CETIP and REUNE transaction scopes.
- ANBIMA, REUNE methodology / FAQ, distinction between total and
  extragroup secondary-market transactions.

bond-market liquidity while preserving the exact WP-06B trading-day
share definition.

Its very high agreement with B3CT during the common Sep-2023-Jun-2025
window supports its use as the historical liquidity candidate for the
next controlled econometric comparison.
