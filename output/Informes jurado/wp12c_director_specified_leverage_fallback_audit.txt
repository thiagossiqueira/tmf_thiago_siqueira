TFM - WP-12C DIRECTOR-SPECIFIED LEVERAGE FALLBACK AUDIT
=============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Implement and audit the leverage hierarchy explicitly requested by thesis
director Martin Grandes on 22-Oct-2026:

1. Preferred concept:
   Market-value debt / market capitalization.

2. If unavailable:
   Total Debt / Total Assets from balance sheets as the explicit second-best
   fallback.

No third proxy is introduced and no specification choice is based on
coefficient sign or statistical significance.


FROZEN ECONOMETRIC ARCHITECTURE
-------------------------------
Dependent variable:
- Geometric corporate spread.

Estimator:
- Bond fixed effects.
- Driscoll-Kraay standard errors.
- Maximum DK lag = 4.

Final table:
- M1-M8 architecture preserved.
- Bond Trading Days excluded from preferred main table.
- Operating Cash Flow / Assets included only in M8.
- Total Debt / EBITDA excluded.
- No Amount Issued / Assets.
- Sovereign risk remains the BRL sovereign spread.

Frozen panel:
- 4,648 bond-month observations.
- 103 bonds.
- 48 issuers.
- 2010m12-2025m6.


DIRECTOR'S PREFERRED MEASURE: MARKET-VALUE DEBT / MARKET CAP
------------------------------------------------------------
The exact preferred measure could not be implemented defensibly from the
available dataset.

The existing WP-12A measure is:

    Book Total Debt / Equity Market Capitalization

This is NOT market-value debt / market capitalization.

It combines a book-value numerator with a market-value denominator and is
therefore retained only as a leverage robustness specification.

WP-12A coverage:
- 3,303 observations.
- 93 bonds.
- 44 issuers.
- 2011m3-2025m6.
- Approximately 71.1% of the frozen panel.

The reduced coverage reflects the requirement for an observable equity market
capitalization and therefore excludes observations associated with issuers
without usable market-cap data. Using WP-12A as the main leverage measure
would consequently change the estimation sample and introduce an avoidable
listed-firm / market-cap availability selection channel.

Coverage on the frozen thesis panel:
- 4,648 / 4,648 observations.

- 103 bonds.
- 48 issuers.
- 2010m12-2025m6.

Observed distribution on the full panel:
- Minimum: 2.0881808938

LINEAGE CROSS-CHECK
-------------------
The direct Bloomberg field was independently compared against:

    100 * SHORT_AND_LONG_TERM_DEBT / BS_TOT_ASSET

using the same frozen point-in-time accounting quarter.

On all 4,648 thesis observations:
- Direct field nonmissing: 4,648.
- Constructed comparator nonmissing: 4,648.
- Absolute difference <= 0.01 percentage points: 4,648 / 4,648.
- Observations with difference > 0.01 pp: 0.
- Maximum absolute difference: 0.0004333262 pp.

Conclusion:
- Bloomberg TOT_DEBT_TO_TOT_ASSET is accepted as the main implementation.
- The reconstructed ratio is retained only as a lineage validation.

CONTROLLED WP-12A VS WP-12C LEVERAGE-PROXY BRIDGE
--------------------------------------------------
To separate the effect of the leverage definition from the effect of the
estimation sample, M4, M5, M6 and M8 were re-estimated on the exact same
WP-12A sample:

- 3,303 observations.
- 93 bonds.
- 44 issuers.
- 2011m3-2025m6.
- Bond FE.
- Driscoll-Kraay lag(4).

Within each model pair, every regressor and observation is identical.
Only the leverage proxy changes.

Controlled Total Debt / Total Assets results:

M4:
- coefficient = +1.6634
- DK SE = 1.0103
- p = 0.102

M5:
- coefficient = +1.3658
- DK SE = 1.0228
- p = 0.184

M6:
- coefficient = +0.3463
- DK SE = 1.0883
- p = 0.751

M8:
- coefficient = +1.6759
- DK SE = 1.0097
- p = 0.099

Therefore the negative coefficient observed in the final 4,648-observation
WP-12C table is NOT mechanically generated by replacing Book Debt / Market
Cap with Total Debt / Total Assets.

Holding the sample fixed at 3,303 observations, Total Debt / Total Assets is
positive in all four specifications.

The sign reversal occurs when the Total Debt / Total Assets measure permits
the estimation sample to expand from 3,303 to the full 4,648 observations.

Interpretation:
- This is a sample-composition / coverage sensitivity.
- It must not be interpreted as evidence for selecting one proxy based on
  coefficient sign.
- The final proxy choice remains governed by the director's stated hierarchy
  and data defensibility.


RELATION BETWEEN THE TWO LEVERAGE PROXIES
-----------------------------------------
On the identical 3,303-observation controlled sample:

Book Debt / Equity Market Cap:
- Mean = 0.8315243
- SD = 0.6787127
- Min = 0.0580501
- Max = 9.549443

Total Debt / Total Assets:
- Mean = 36.17349
- SD = 14.10556
- Min = 2.088181
- Max = 79.53697

Contemporaneous correlation:
- rho = 0.3046
- p = 0.0000
- N = 3,303

The two measures are positively related but only moderately correlated.
They therefore capture related but materially different leverage concepts,
and their coefficient magnitudes should not be compared directly because
their economic definitions and scales differ.


DECISION
--------
PASS - Adopt Total Debt / Total Assets as the main leverage proxy.

Reasons:
1. It is the explicit second-best measure specified by the director when
   market-value debt / market cap is unavailable.
2. The exact preferred market-value debt numerator is not available
   defensibly in the frozen dataset.
3. Bloomberg TOT_DEBT_TO_TOT_ASSET provides 100% coverage of the frozen panel.
4. The direct Bloomberg field is independently validated against the
   reconstructed balance-sheet ratio.
5. It preserves the complete 4,648-observation thesis panel.
6. The choice is made ex ante from the director's hierarchy and data quality,
   not from coefficient sign or statistical significance.

Book Total Debt / Equity Market Cap remains in the robustness annex (WP-12A)
because:
- it is a useful market-denominator leverage robustness measure;
- it is NOT the requested market-value debt / market-cap concept;
- it has materially lower coverage;
- adopting it as baseline would impose a market-cap availability selection.

DEFENSE TAKEAWAY
----------------
If questioned during the defense:

- The director first requested market-value debt / market capitalization.
- The available market-cap implementation uses BOOK debt in the numerator,
  so it is not the requested concept and is retained transparently as a
  robustness exercise.
- The director explicitly specified Total Debt / Total Assets as the fallback.
- Bloomberg provides that measure directly with 100% panel coverage, and it
  reproduces the independently reconstructed balance-sheet ratio almost
  exactly.
- A controlled common-sample exercise shows that the different leverage
  results are sensitive to sample composition; therefore no proxy was chosen
  because it produced a preferred coefficient sign.
